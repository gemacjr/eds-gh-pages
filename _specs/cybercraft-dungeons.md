## Problem

A gift site for a teenager who loves Transformers, Pokémon, and video games. The site should be
a game, not a page about games.

## Goals

- A playable Three.js game in the browser
- A futuristic, Cybertron-city palette
- A Minecraft Dungeons–style crawler with Bumblebee and Optimus Prime as playable heroes

## Controls

WASD to move, mouse to aim, left click for melee, right click to shoot, Space to dodge, E for a
potion, 1–3 for artifacts, I for inventory, M for the map. The best floor reached is saved on the
device.

## Iterations

1. The first version was a Transformers + Pokémon game
2. Pokémon were removed to focus on Autobots vs Decepticons, with a more detailed, researched look
3. Added the dungeon crawler, then reworked it to feel closer to the real Minecraft Dungeons
